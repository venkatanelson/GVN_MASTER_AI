2. **Morning Candle Signature:** Check 9:15 AM candle structure and volume pressure to identify institutional bias.
3. **Strong Side Identifier:** The strong side option opens low (0.7-0.6 levels) and prints a long full-bodied green candle.
4. **Weak Side Identifier:** The weak side option opens high (0.3-0.2 levels) and prints a red candle cutting down below 0.5.
5. **Volume Pressure Rule:** Strong side candle must close above the 0.6 GVN level with high volume (2x margin volume).
6. **Asymmetric Touch Rule:** If PE touches/crosses 0.6 or 0.7 but CE fails to reach its level, pressure is locked on the PE side.
6b. **OI % Asymmetry Surge Rule:** If Put Option strikes surge +2,000% to +7,000%+ in OI Change with Put price decay, while Call side unwinds, lock +100% Bullish Wind.
7. **Trend Confirmation (The 0.5 Rule):** The dominant morning trend is fully confirmed only when the strong option holds above 0.5.
8. **Anti-Trap Filter:** Strictly block any trades on the opposite option as long as the dominant option trades above 0.5.
9. **Zero-to-Hero (Z2H) Expiry Rule:** Eligible only on Expiry Days for option contracts with Delta between 0.40 and 0.85.
10. **Z2H Watchlist Qualification:** The option contract qualifies if its 9:15 AM candle low drops below Level i7 (0.220 Fib).
11. **Z2H Entry Zone:** Buy when price retraces near Level i1 (1.0 Fib / Bottom level) with a ±3.0 point buffer.
12. **Z2H Wind Direction Sync:** Z2H entry is allowed ONLY if the corresponding Delta-60 option is trading above its 0.6 & 0.5 levels.
13. **Z2H Anti-Trap Sync:** Block Z2H entries if `TRAP` or `PREMIUM EATING` is detected in the wind direction.
14. **Z2H Targets:** Target 1 is Level i7 (0.220 Fib), Target 2 is Level i6 (0.382 Fib), and Target 3 is Level i5 (0.50 Fib).
15. **Afternoon Reversal Watch (12:30 PM+):** Watch for reversals if the morning trend starts to exhaust.
16. **Reversal Trigger 1 (Index):** Main Index Spot must cross its key 0.618 Fibonacci level from below.
17. **Reversal Trigger 2 (Option):** The dormant opposite Delta-60 option must cross its 0.6 GVN level from below.
18. **Reversal Confirmation:** Reversal is fully active once the opposite option crosses and holds above its 0.5 GVN level.
19. **Reversal Targets:** Buy opposite option between 0.6 and 0.5 levels, targeting Level 0.3 (0.382 Fib) and above.
20. **Risk Management:** Strict 12.0 point Stop Loss for all setups; automatic square-off at market close.
