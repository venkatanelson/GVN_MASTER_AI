7. **Trend Confirmation (The 0.5 Sync):** Confirmed when Index breaks 0.5 and the strong option holds above 0.5 GVN level.

13. **Afternoon Reversal Option Sign:** Dormant opposite Delta-60 option must cross 0.6 GVN level from below and hold 0.5.
14. **Z2H Expiry Day Multiplier:** Expiry options at i1 near support expand 3x-5x as Gamma peaks at 0.5 and Delta nears 1.0.
